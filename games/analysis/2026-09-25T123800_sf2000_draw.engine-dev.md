## 2. Diagnosis — the root causes that cost the most

1. **Missing king-safety evaluation + missing check extensions → the engine repeatedly walks its own king into forced-tactic territory.** Five of the seventeen flagged blunders (plies 47, 83, 87, 113, 117) and both mate-score swings trace directly to this. At 5 s/move with no transposition table, the search reaches roughly depth 6–8 in a busy middlegame; forcing sequences that need 8+ plies of checks/discovered attacks to refute are simply outside the search horizon, and nothing in the static eval discourages the exposed king square in the meantime. This is the single most expensive class of mistake in the game (it alone accounts for most of the inflated 228 cp/move average).
2. **Shallow effective search depth from the missing TT, killer/history ordering, and PVS/LMR.** This explains the broad background of "ordinary" -100 to -250 cp losses (plies 57, 67, 79, 109, 203, 205) where no tactical trick is involved — the engine just doesn't calculate deep enough to find the objectively best move. This compounds directly with root cause #1: more depth is exactly what's needed to see king-safety threats before they land.
3. **v1's material-only evaluation explains essentially all of the opening-phase blunders** (plies 13, 17, 19, 23, 25, 39, 43): moves like f3, g3, a4, Qa2 cost zero material, so a material-only eval treats them as equal to principled developing moves. This is already superseded by v2/v3's PeSTO PSTs going forward — flagged here for completeness, not as new work, but worth a quick spot-check (see Risks) since search depth still has to be sufficient to actually find the now-PST-favored move.

## 3. Improvement plan

Ranked by expected Elo gain per hour of work. Items already in the JOURNAL.md queue are marked; two new items (3 and 5) are proposed based on this game's evidence.

1. **Check extensions on checks (capped) + `score mate N` in UCI output** *(already queued)*
   - Implement: in `Search::negamax`, when the side to move is in check, search that node without decrementing depth (extend by 1 ply), bounded by a per-branch extension counter (e.g. max +8 ply total) so a long check chain can't blow the search open. Also convert huge cp scores to `score mate N` in the `info depth ...` line printed from `Search::best_move`.
   - Where: `engine/src/main.rs`, `Search::negamax` (the recursive depth argument) and `Search::best_move`'s `println!`.
   - Elo estimate: **+50 to +90**. Directly removes this game's two largest single blunders (ply 47, ply 113 — both mate-score swings caused by not seeing a forced sequence through checks).
   - Test: add a `#[cfg(test)]` case reproducing the ply 45–47 FEN (after `Bxd3`) and assert `best_move` picks `Kd2` over `Ke2`; then A/B gauntlet vs current build.

2. **Transposition table + hash-move ordering** *(already queued)*
   - Implement: `HashMap<u64, TTEntry { depth, score, flag, best_move }>` keyed by the existing `hash(pos)`; probe at the top of `negamax` for a cutoff or to seed move ordering with the stored best move; store an entry on exit.
   - Where: `engine/src/main.rs`, new `TTEntry`/table on `Search`, hooked into `negamax` and the per-depth loop in `best_move`.
   - Elo estimate: **+80 to +150**. Foundational — lets iterative deepening reuse prior work and cuts node counts sharply, raising effective depth within the fixed 5 s budget. This is the direct fix for root cause #2 (the background of "ordinary" shallow-search losses).
   - Test: at a fixed mid-game FEN, compare the depth reached in a 5 s search before/after (expect a meaningful increase); then gauntlet.

3. **King-danger evaluation term (attacker count + open lines to king), not just a pawn shield** *(new; extends queued item "king safety (pawn shield)")*
   - Implement: a new `king_danger(pos, color) -> i32` in `eval.rs` scoring (a) semi-open/open files toward the king occupied by enemy rooks/queens, and (b) a weighted count of enemy piece pseudo-attacks landing on the king's surrounding squares (classic danger-index → penalty curve). Fold the (tapered) result into `evaluate()` alongside the existing PST accumulation.
   - Where: `engine/src/eval.rs`, `evaluate()` plus the new `king_danger()` helper.
   - Elo estimate: **+30 to +55**. Targets exactly the ply 47/83/87/113/117 pattern: the static eval never penalized the exposed `Ke2`/`Kf1`/`Kf3` squares, so the search had no positional signal steering it away even at the depths it could reach.
   - Test: `eval.rs` unit test asserting the ply 45 FEN scores `Kd2` better than `Ke2` at depth 0 (static eval alone), matching the direction of the observed -8967 cp swing; then gauntlet.

4. **Killer moves + history heuristic** *(already queued)*
   - Implement: `killers[ply][2]` storing the last two quiet moves that caused a beta cutoff, tried right after the TT move and captures; `history[color][from][to]` incremented on cutoff, used as the ordering tiebreak for remaining quiet moves (replacing the current pure `sort_by_key(mvv_lva)`).
   - Where: `engine/src/main.rs`, `Search` struct + the move-ordering step in `negamax`.
   - Elo estimate: **+30 to +55**. Compounds with the TT to raise effective depth further; helps the plain shallow-search losses (ply 57, 67, 109, 203, 205).
   - Test: node-count reduction across a fixed suite built from this game's flagged-mistake positions; then gauntlet.

5. **Quiescence: search non-capturing checks at the first ply only** *(new, small)*
   - Implement: in `Search::quiesce`, when not already in check, also generate and search legal moves that give check but aren't captures — restricted to the entry quiescence ply only (not recursively), so the cost is bounded to one extra "checking ply." Deeper qs plies keep the current captures/promotions-only filter.
   - Where: `engine/src/main.rs`, `Search::quiesce`'s move filter.
   - Elo estimate: **+10 to +20**. Small but cheap; catches the "quiet check wins material shortly after" shape that neither MVV-LVA quiescence nor a depth-limited main search sees at the horizon.
   - Test: a constructed FEN where a quiet check forces material loss one move past the current horizon — verify the eval only flips correctly after the change; gauntlet with node-count monitoring (highest risk item for the 5 s budget — see Risks).

6. **Null-move pruning** *(already queued)*
   - Implement: standard R=2/3 reduction, disabled when in check or in low non-pawn-material endgames (to avoid zugzwang errors).
   - Where: `engine/src/main.rs`, `Search::negamax`.
   - Elo estimate: **+30 to +50**.
   - Test: verify no regressions on known zugzwang FENs (K+P endgames); then gauntlet.

7. **PVS + LMR** *(already queued)*
   - Implement: full-window search for the first (TT/killer-ordered) move, null-window for the rest with depth reduction on late quiet moves, re-searching on fail-high. Best done after items 2 and 4 (TT + ordering) are in place, since PVS/LMR's value depends on good move ordering — hence ranked after them despite a comparable raw Elo estimate.
   - Where: `engine/src/main.rs`, `Search::negamax`'s move loop.
   - Elo estimate: **+40 to +80** (once items 2/4 exist).
   - Test: gauntlet; verify the reduced search still finds the same best move as an unreduced search on a small tactical test suite (no LMR-induced tactical blindness).

8. **Opening book (~10 ply, generated by full-strength Stockfish)** *(already queued)*
   - Implement: a static table of `position hash -> move` for a handful of solid White lines, consulted before falling back to `best_move` on `go`.
   - Where: new `engine/src/book.rs`, hooked into `main()`'s `"go"` handler.
   - Elo estimate: **+15 to +30** for this specific weakness — v1's opening blunders (ply 13/17/19/23/25) are largely already fixed by v2's PSTs (see Diagnosis #3), so the book's marginal value on top of PSTs is lower than items above; still worth doing for guaranteed sound development at zero search cost.
   - Test: gauntlet games starting from the book lines vs. off-book.

9. **Mop-up + passed pawns + endgame king safety** *(already queued)*
   - Implement: king-to-king distance/opposition bonus scaled by material imbalance in the endgame phase, plus a passed-pawn bonus by rank; share the tapering logic with item 3's king-danger term since they live in the same eval area.
   - Where: `engine/src/eval.rs`, `evaluate()` (endgame-phase-weighted terms).
   - Elo estimate: **+15 to +25** for this game specifically — the endgame here ended in a repetition draw rather than a conversion failure, so the payoff is more general than this-game-specific.
   - Test: standard mop-up positions (K+Q vs K, K+R vs K) converge to mate quickly; then gauntlet.

## 4. Risks

- **Check extensions and the quiescence quiet-check addition must be capped** (max total extension depth per branch; quiet checks searched only at the first quiescence ply, not recursively), or a checky position could expand the tree enough to threaten the **5 s/move hard limit**. The existing deadline check only fires every 2048 nodes (`self.nodes & 2047 == 0`) — a runaway check-extension chain could still overshoot noticeably before the next check. Test explicitly against worst-case checky positions (e.g. a position with a long forced-check sequence) with a wall-clock assertion, not just node counts.
- **TT correctness**: a hash hit must not surface a `bestmove` or `info score` from a search that was itself cut short by `self.stopped` — returning an unverified stale move from a shallower/earlier search would be a UCI-correctness regression, not just a strength regression.
- **Null-move pruning** must be disabled when in check and in low-material (pawn+king) endgames to avoid classic zugzwang blunders; verify against known zugzwang FENs before promoting.
- **The new king-danger eval term must be tapered by game phase** alongside the (already-queued) mop-up/endgame term — an untapered penalty for an "exposed" king would misfire once queens are off and the king should actively centralize. Also re-run the existing `eval.rs` symmetry tests (`eval_is_symmetric_for_side_to_move`, `startpos_is_balanced`, `extra_queen_is_worth_a_lot`) after adding it — an asymmetric king-safety computation for White vs Black would silently bias play and might not be caught by a gauntlet alone.
- As always: every change here goes through the existing A/B gauntlet (`uv run python -m arena.gauntlet`) before touching `bin/destroyer-official` via `scripts/promote.sh`, so the always-on campaign runner is never disrupted mid-game.
