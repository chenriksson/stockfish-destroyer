# Engine-dev analysis: 2026-09-25T123624_sf2000_draw

**Result**: 1/2-1/2 (threefold repetition), White = Destroyer 0.2.0 (engine-v1), Black = Stockfish 17.1 @ 2000, 133 plies, 5 s/move, Apple M5 Pro.
**Engine that played**: engine-v1 (commit 2c0be6e) — material-only eval, no PSTs, no repetition detection, no TT/killers/PVS/LMR. Improvement targets below are aimed at the **current** source (engine-v3: `engine/src/main.rs`, `engine/src/eval.rs` — tapered PeSTO eval + repetition/50-move/insufficient-material draw detection + 30 cp contempt).

Context: full-strength Stockfish had this position at −600 to −1600 cp (i.e. crushing) for most of the game after move ~14, yet it ended in a draw only because Stockfish-at-2000 gave back huge chunks of its advantage repeatedly (e.g. move 54 Rxd2 instead of Qg1+: +413 cp back to us; move 94 f5 instead of Qg3+: +445 cp back; move 110 Qc3 instead of Qg3+: +747 cp back). This game is a near-loss salvaged by the opponent's own rating cap, not a game we were ever close to winning — treat the "draw" label with that caveat.

## 2. Diagnosis — root causes that cost the most

1. **Search is far too shallow for the 5 s budget** — no transposition table, no killer/history move ordering, no PVS/LMR, no check extensions. This is the single biggest bucket: moves 14, 46, 54, 55, 57 (5 of 7 blunders, **−1338 cp, 84% of the total loss**) are all cases where a search that reached 1–2 ply further in the same wall-clock time would have found either the opponent's tactic (move 14) or a better defensive try (moves 46/54/55/57). Everything queued as items 2, 4, 6 in JOURNAL.md (TT, killer/history, PVS+LMR) attacks exactly this, and this game is strong evidence to prioritize them.
2. **No king-safety evaluation term** — move 25 (Bxg7, −106 cp) is a pure positional blunder: the eval has no penalty for a king still on its home square next to enemy heavy pieces, so a "free" pawn on g7 looks better than tucking the king in with Ke1. This also compounds cause #1: without a static king-danger price, the search has to *calculate* every king-safety tactic move-by-move instead of pricing exposure in immediately, which is much more expensive and exactly why move 14's combo was missed.
3. **(Already being fixed) Opening judgment** — the first 9 moves (a3-b3-c3-d3-e3-f3-g3-h3, no piece developed, king never moved) cost ~300 cp before move 10 even happened, purely from v1's material-only eval having zero development incentive. `engine-v2`'s tapered PeSTO PSTs directly fix the symptom (central squares now score higher than rim squares for minors); an opening book (queued item 1) is still worth having but is now a smaller marginal fix than causes #1–2.

Note on the draw itself: v1 has no repetition detection at all, so heading into the threefold repetition wasn't a deliberate choice — it's the arena's normal rules enforcement kicking in while White made "least-bad" quiet bishop moves (Bf1/Be2) and Black's king/pawn shuffled too. `engine-v3`'s repetition-aware contempt (disabled below −300 eval) means the *current* build would now actively steer toward a repetition when this far behind, which is correct behavior — not something to change.

## 3. Improvement plan (ranked by expected Elo gain per hour)

### 1. Minimal king-danger eval term — NEW, cheap slice of queued item 7
- **What**: a small `king_danger()` term in `eval.rs` that penalizes a king still on its home square (e1/e8) while the opponent's queen is on the board and enough material has developed (use existing `phase` — e.g. once `phase >= 8`). Start with a flat penalty (e.g. −10 to −50 cp scaled linearly by phase), added into `mg[side]` after the per-piece loop in `evaluate()` (it's tapered away naturally since queens are usually off by the endgame). This directly targets moves like 25 (Bxg7).
- **Where**: `engine/src/eval.rs::evaluate()`, new `fn king_danger(pos: &Chess, color: Color, phase: i32) -> i32`.
- **Elo estimate**: +20–30 Elo. Effort: ~30 min.
- **Test**: add a unit test alongside the existing ones (`king_stuck_in_center_with_queens_is_penalized`, mirroring `developed_knight_beats_rim_knight`'s symmetry pattern — verify White-to-move-e1-with-queens scores worse than castled-g1-with-queens, and that the term is symmetric for both colors like `eval_is_symmetric_for_side_to_move`). Confirm with an A/B gauntlet (`uv run python -m arena.gauntlet bin/destroyer bin/destroyer-<tag>`) against the previous build.

### 2. Killer moves + history heuristic — already queued (item 4)
- **What**: `killers: [[Option<Move>; 2]; MAX_PLY as usize]` (2 killers per ply) and `history: [[i32; 64]; 64]` (from×to) on `Search`. Order moves: hash move (once TT exists) → captures by MVV-LVA → killers[ply] → remaining quiet moves sorted by `history[from][to]` descending. On a beta cutoff by a quiet (non-capture) move, push it into `killers[ply]` and bump `history[m.from()][m.to()] += (depth*depth) as i32`.
- **Where**: `engine/src/main.rs::Search` (new fields) and the `ordered.sort_by_key(...)` calls in `negamax`/`quiesce`.
- **Elo estimate**: +50–70 Elo. Effort: ~1.25 h.
- **Test**: nodes-to-depth should drop noticeably at fixed time (log `info depth`/`nodes` and compare same positions before/after); then A/B gauntlet.

### 3. Transposition table + hash-move ordering — already queued (item 2)
- **What**: fixed-size array (e.g. `1 << 20` entries, index `hash & mask`) of `{key: u64, depth: u8, score: i32, bound: Exact/Lower/Upper, best: Option<Move>}`. Probe at the top of `negamax` before move generation; on a sufficient-depth hit with the right bound, return/tighten alpha-beta immediately. Always try the stored `best` move first in ordering (re-validate it's actually legal in the current position before playing it — collisions happen). Store on exit, but **only if `!self.stopped`** (never persist a truncated/timed-out score).
- **Where**: `engine/src/main.rs::Search` (new `tt: Vec<Option<TTEntry>>` field), `negamax()` probe/store, `best_move()` to seed/read the root TT move.
- **Elo estimate**: +90–150 Elo (biggest single lever — this game's 5 missed-tactic blunders are exactly what deeper/faster search fixes). Effort: ~2.5 h, mostly the mate-score ply-adjustment (see Risks).
- **Test**: same nodes/depth-at-fixed-time comparison as above, then full gauntlet; also re-run this exact game's position at move 14 (`fen_before` for ply 27) with `go movetime 5000` on old vs new binary and confirm the new one finds `Nf3` (or at least scores dxe4 much lower) at a comparable or greater depth.

### 4. Null-move pruning — already queued (item 5)
- **What**: at nodes with `depth >= 3`, not in check, `ply > 0`, and the side to move has non-pawn material (skip in pawn/king-only endings — see Risks), search a reduced-depth null move (`R = 2`) with a null window `(-beta, -beta+1)`; if it still fails high, prune.
- **Where**: `engine/src/main.rs::Search::negamax`, right after the repetition/draw checks. Needs a "give the opponent the move" position — shakmaty's `Chess` won't accept an illegal null move directly, so build it via `Fen`/`Setup` (flip side to move, clear en-passant square, keep everything else) rather than `play_unchecked`.
- **Elo estimate**: +25–35 Elo. Effort: ~45 min (plus the null-move-position plumbing).
- **Test**: implement after item 3 so there's a TT baseline to compare node counts/scores against; verify no regression on the R vs Q and K+P endgame-style positions from this game's tail (moves 90+) via the gauntlet, since those are exactly the low-material positions where a naive null-move implementation misplays zugzwang.

### 5. Check extensions + quiescence bad-capture (SEE) pruning + `score mate N` — already queued (item 3)
- **What**: (a) extend search depth by +1 (capped at +2 total per line) when the move just played gives check — this is what would have let the search see the move-14 …Qh4+/…d3+ combo at nominal depth; (b) add a simple SEE (`piece_value`-based) to skip clearly losing captures in `quiesce`, speeding it up; (c) once mate-range scores appear (`alpha.abs() > MATE - MAX_PLY`), print `info ... score mate N` (sign relative to side-to-move, distance = `(MATE - alpha.abs() + 1) / 2`) instead of `score cp`.
- **Where**: `engine/src/main.rs::Search::negamax` (extension), `::quiesce` (SEE-based filter on the existing `ordered` capture list), `::best_move`/UCI print in `main()`.
- **Elo estimate**: +35–55 Elo. Effort: ~1.5 h.
- **Test**: gauntlet; also construct a small forced-mate FEN and confirm `score mate N` appears with the correct sign both when we're mating and (via a losing FEN) when we're being mated.

### 6. PVS + LMR — already queued (item 6)
- **What**: search the first move at each node with a full window, subsequent moves with a null window (re-search full on fail-high); reduce depth for late, quiet, non-check moves (LMR) once ordering (items 2–3) makes "late" mean "actually less promising."
- **Where**: `engine/src/main.rs::Search::negamax` move loop.
- **Elo estimate**: +25–40 Elo. Effort: ~1 h. Implement after items 2–3 — LMR on top of today's ordering-free move loop is riskier (reduces moves almost at random).
- **Test**: gauntlet; watch for tactical regressions specifically on positions like move 14's (forcing lines with checks) since LMR must not reduce checking moves.

### 7. Opening book — already queued (item 1)
- **What**: a small hardcoded `position (as UCI move prefix) → move` table for ~6–8 plies of solid White lines (generate with full-strength `bin/stockfish` at high depth), consulted in the `go` handler before invoking search when we're still in book.
- **Where**: new lookup in `main()`'s `Some("go")` arm, gated on `history.len()` / move number.
- **Elo estimate**: +15–25 Elo (lower now that PST already fixed the worst symptom of this game's opening). Effort: ~1 h.
- **Test**: play a few `arena` smoke games and confirm book moves are used and legal; gauntlet for overall Elo.

### 8. Full king-safety (pawn shield) / mop-up / passed pawns — remainder of already-queued item 7
- **What**: the fuller version of item 1 above — pawn-shield integrity, open/half-open files toward the king, passed-pawn bonuses, king-to-passed-pawn distance in the endgame (mop-up).
- **Where**: `engine/src/eval.rs::evaluate()`.
- **Elo estimate**: +15–25 Elo. Effort: ~1 h. Lower priority than the minimal slice (item 1) given the 2-day budget — do this only if time remains after items 1–7.
- **Test**: gauntlet; spot-check the endgame positions from this game's tail (moves 90+) for more sensible king activity.

## 4. Risks

- **TT mate-score adjustment**: mate scores must be adjusted by `ply` when stored/retrieved (store as "distance to mate from this node," reconstruct at the probing node's ply) — the classic source of wrong mate claims or search instability. Add a dedicated test.
- **TT collisions**: always re-validate a TT-suggested move is legal in the current position before playing it; a bare `play_unchecked` on a stale/colliding entry can misplay or panic.
- **Never store from a truncated search**: `negamax`/`quiesce` currently return `0` the instant `self.stopped` is set (checked immediately after every recursive call before the score is used) — this pattern must be preserved for every new recursive call site (null-move, check-extension, LMR re-search) and TT stores must be skipped whenever `self.stopped` is true, or a timed-out search corrupts future probes.
- **Null-move zugzwang**: several of this game's own positions (moves 90+) are exactly the low-material K+P / single-minor endings where null-move pruning is unsound. Guard it on non-pawn material for the side to move, and disable in check.
- **Check-extension blowup**: cap total extensions per line (not just per node) — an uncapped forcing sequence (this game had long check chains, e.g. moves 27–35) could burn the whole 5 s budget on one line and starve the rest of the move list at the current iterative-deepening depth, without technically breaking the 5 s cap (the existing `nodes & 2047` time check still fires) but effectively wasting the search.
- **`score mate N` sign**: must be relative to the side to move in the UCI sense, not our internal negamax sign — easy to invert and report a mate for the wrong side, which is a real UCI-correctness bug (not just an Elo issue) since GUIs/arbiters may use it.
- **Ply-indexed arrays (killers) vs `MAX_PLY = 96`**: check extensions push real recursion depth up; index `killers[ply]` with `ply.min(MAX_PLY as usize - 1)` (or grow `MAX_PLY`) rather than trusting `ply` stays in bounds.
- **Opening book legality**: re-validate a book move against `legal_moves()` before playing it (a stale/mistyped book entry must never be trusted blindly) — falling back to search on any mismatch.
- **None of the above should touch `think_time()` / the 5 s cap** — all of items 1–8 are pure search-quality/eval changes; nothing here needs (or should get) more time per move.
